### 1. Renaissance noble portrait

```text
Using the uploaded pet photo as the reference, keep the exact breed, fur colour, markings and eye colour. A regal Renaissance-style oil-painting portrait of the pet as a nobleman, wearing a deep purple velvet coat with gold embroidery and a white ruffled collar, sitting upright with a dignified expression. Dark background, dramatic Rembrandt lighting, rich brushwork and varnish texture, museum-quality, 4:5.
```
