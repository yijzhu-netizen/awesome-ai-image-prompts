# Couple photos prompts

Matching avatars, engagement-style portraits and cinematic moments for two. Upload a photo of each person (with both people's OK).

**Negative prompt for every prompt on this page** (paste it into the negative-prompt field, or add it as "Avoid: …"):

```text
extra people, merged or fused bodies, mismatched faces, changed identity, distorted hands, extra fingers, blurry faces, awkward anatomy, text, watermark, logo, nsfw
```

## Prompts (12)

### 1. Matching anime couple avatars

```text
Using the two reference photos, draw Person A and Person B as anime characters in a soft Japanese animation style, keeping each person's hairstyle, hair colour and face shape. One wide 2:1 image split into two halves: Person A on the left half facing right, Person B on the right half facing left, both smiling slightly as if looking at each other. Shared continuous background of a pastel sky with a few cherry blossom petals crossing the middle, matching colours and line style on both sides, each half works as a square profile picture.
```

### 2. Golden-hour field

```text
Romantic photo of Person A and Person B from the reference photos, keeping both faces recognisable, walking hand in hand through a tall golden grass field at sunset, Person B laughing and looking at Person A. Warm backlight with gentle sun flare, light breeze moving hair and clothes, casual linen outfits in cream and soft blue, 50mm, f/2, natural candid feel, exactly two people.
```

### 3. Rainy night, one umbrella

```text
Cinematic photo of Person A and Person B from the reference photos sharing one clear umbrella on a rainy city street at night, standing close, Person A looking at Person B with a soft smile. Purple and cyan bokeh lights in the background, wet pavement reflections, light rain, plain knit sweaters, 85mm, f/1.8, film-like colour grade, tender mood, keep both faces true to the references.
```

### 4. Pixar-style 3D couple

```text
Pixar-style 3D animated characters of Person A and Person B, based on the reference photos: same hairstyles, hair colours, glasses or other signature details, big expressive eyes, warm smiles. They sit side by side on a cosy sofa sharing a bowl of popcorn, soft warm lamp light, pastel living room, high-quality 3D render, subsurface skin, cute and heart-warming.
```

### 5. Retro instant photo

```text
Retro instant-film photo of Person A and Person B from the reference photos, cheek to cheek, making a playful face at the camera, taken with direct on-camera flash at night. Slight overexposure on the faces, dark background, warm faded colours, soft vignette, visible film grain, white instant-film border, nostalgic 1990s feel, both faces recognisable.
```

### 6. Hanfu couple in a courtyard

```text
Portrait of Person A and Person B from the reference photos wearing elegant Song-dynasty style hanfu in soft jade and ivory silk, standing under a blooming plum tree in a traditional Chinese courtyard with grey tiled roofs. Person A holds an oil-paper umbrella over both of them, gentle smiles, soft overcast light, muted classical colour palette, 85mm, refined guofeng atmosphere, faces true to the references.
```

### 7. Studio engagement portrait

```text
Elegant studio engagement portrait of Person A and Person B from the reference photos, Person B resting a hand on Person A's shoulder, both looking at the camera with relaxed smiles. Neutral warm-grey seamless backdrop, soft large key light, refined classic outfits in black and white, 85mm, f/5.6, timeless and clean, natural skin texture, exactly two people.
```

### 8. Two halves of one sunset (avatars)

```text
Matching couple profile pictures from the two reference photos, illustrated in a clean flat digital-painting style that keeps each person's hairstyle and features. One wide 2:1 image: Person A on the left looking toward the centre, Person B on the right looking toward the centre, a large setting sun sits exactly in the middle so each half shows half of the sun. Same warm orange-to-violet sky on both halves, simple and bold, each half crops into a square avatar.
```

### 9. Cosy kitchen candid

```text
Candid lifestyle photo of Person A and Person B from the reference photos cooking together in a small sunny kitchen, Person A stirring a pot while Person B laughs and steals a taste, flour on the counter, plants on the windowsill. Bright morning light, warm natural colours, 35mm, documentary style, genuine moment, both faces recognisable.
```

### 10. Lantern-lit old town walk

```text
Travel photo of Person A and Person B from the reference photos strolling through a narrow old-town street at dusk lit by warm paper lanterns, seen from slightly behind and to the side as Person A turns back to smile at the camera. Stone paving, wooden shop fronts without readable signs, soft warm glow, 35mm, f/2, cinematic travel-vlog look.
```

### 11. Chibi sticker couple

```text
Cute chibi sticker of Person A and Person B based on the reference photos, big heads and small bodies, keeping their hairstyles, hair colours and any glasses, Person B hugging Person A from the side, both blushing and smiling. Thick white sticker outline, flat pastel colours, small floating hearts, plain white background, kawaii style.
```

### 12. Long-distance split screen

```text
Split-screen image for a long-distance couple: left half shows Person A from the reference photos at a desk by a window at night with city lights, right half shows Person B from the reference photos by a window in the morning with soft sunlight. Both hold a phone and smile at it, a thin glowing line connects the two halves in the middle. Cinematic, gentle colour contrast of cool night and warm morning, faces true to the references.
```
