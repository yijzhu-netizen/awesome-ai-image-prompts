# Headshots prompts

Turn one clear selfie into a believable LinkedIn, CV or team-page headshot. The prompts change light, wardrobe and backdrop, never your face.

**Negative prompt for every prompt on this page** (paste it into the negative-prompt field, or add it as "Avoid: …"):

```text
cartoon, illustration, plastic or over-smoothed skin, distorted face, changed identity, asymmetrical eyes, extra fingers, crooked teeth, heavy makeup, harsh shadows, busy background, text, watermark, logo
```

## Prompts (12)

### 1. Classic corporate, grey backdrop

```text
Using the uploaded photo as the face reference, keep the person's face, hairline, skin tone and features exactly the same. Professional corporate headshot, head and shoulders, wearing a tailored charcoal suit jacket over a plain white shirt, confident relaxed smile, looking straight at the camera. Seamless mid-grey studio backdrop, soft key light from the front-left with a gentle fill, subtle hair light, 85mm lens, f/4, sharp eyes, natural skin texture, square 1:1 crop.
```
