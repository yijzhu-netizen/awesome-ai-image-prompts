# Store & e-commerce images prompts

Product photos and banners that look like one photoshoot: Amazon main images, A+ and Brand Store banners, Shopify heroes, Etsy covers and sale sets. Replace [PRODUCT] with your item.

> Source: **[AI Product Photo & Store Banner Prompts: 49 Prompts for Amazon, Shopify, Etsy](https://drawcue.com/ai-store-design/)** on drawcue.com, with previews, a step-by-step guide and FAQ. Licensed [CC BY 4.0](../LICENSE): please credit drawcue.com.

**Amazon main-image rules** (check Seller Central before you list, since rules change):

- Pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255).
- The product fills about 85% of the frame; 1600 px or more on the longest side gives the best zoom (1000 px minimum for zoom).
- No added text, logos, badges, borders or props on the main image: only the product being sold.
- It must be a professional photo that accurately shows the actual product. Use the main-image prompt to clean up or restage a photo of your real item (upload it as the reference), and use AI freely for lifestyle, infographic, A+ and Brand Store images.

**Negative prompt for every prompt on this page** (paste it into the negative-prompt field, or add it as "Avoid: …"):

```text
text, letters, numbers, prices, watermark, brand logos, trademarks, distorted product, extra parts, cluttered background, low resolution
```
