# Face swap (consent first) prompts

Put **your own** face into another era, a costume or a dream job. Every prompt starts by locking the identity to your uploaded selfie.

> Source: **[AI Face Swap Prompts: 12 Free, Consent-First Ideas (Yourself in Any Era)](https://drawcue.com/ai-face-swap/)** on drawcue.com, with previews, a step-by-step guide and FAQ. Licensed [CC BY 4.0](../LICENSE): please credit drawcue.com.

**Read before you start:**

- Only use **your own face**, or the face of someone who has clearly agreed to this specific use.
- No celebrities or other real people without consent, and no impersonation: never make it look like someone said, did or endorsed something.
- Never use face swaps for fraud, scams, fake IDs, harassment, revenge or political deception.
- No sexual or nude content of anyone, and **never** any image involving minors.
- Label AI-edited images when you share them. Many platforms and jurisdictions require it, and most AI tools forbid non-consensual face swaps.

**Negative prompt for every prompt on this page** (paste it into the negative-prompt field, or add it as "Avoid: …"):

```text
different person, face of a celebrity, distorted face, mismatched skin tone, visible seam around the face, extra fingers, text, watermark, logo
```
