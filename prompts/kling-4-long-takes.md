## Prompts (10)

### 1. Neon city long take

```text
[Kling 4.0 · 30 seconds · 16:9 · single continuous long take]
Subject: a lone traveler in a long red coat and black boots, short dark hair, carrying a small leather suitcase.
Scene: a rain-soaked neon street in an Asian megacity at dusk, glowing signs, puddles, crowds with umbrellas.
Style & light: cinematic, anamorphic look, teal-and-magenta colour grade, wet reflections, light haze.
Timeline:
- 0–8s: The traveler steps out of a subway exit into the rain and looks up at the towering signs. Camera: extreme wide shot, slow drone descent from rooftop height to street level.
- 8–16s: She walks through the crowd, umbrellas and reflections sliding past. Camera: steadicam tracking from behind, over-the-shoulder, foreground parallax.
- 16–24s: She stops at a small noodle stall, turns, and smiles as the cook waves. Camera: camera arcs around to a medium close-up of her face, shallow depth of field.
- 24–30s: She sits down as steam rises and the street lights flicker on. Camera: slow pull-back and crane up to a wide shot of the street.
Sound: rain on awnings, distant traffic, soft synthwave pad, the cook says 'Welcome back!'.
Constraints: one continuous 30-second take, no cuts, the same character face, hair and outfit from start to finish, natural physics and smooth motion, no on-screen text or watermark.
```

### 2. Product hero reveal

Style & light: premium commercial look, high contrast, crisp reflections, subtle particles in the air.

- 0–8s: The case sits in darkness; a beam of light slowly sweeps across the gold line. Camera: extreme close-up macro, very slow push-in.

- 16–24s: The earbuds rotate in the air showing every angle, light rippling over them. Camera: slow orbit at eye level, shallow depth of field.
- 24–30s: The earbuds settle back, the lid closes with a soft click, light fades to a clean hero pose. Camera: pull back to a centered medium shot, static final frame.
Sound: deep cinematic whoosh, soft click when the lid opens and closes, minimal electronic pulse.
Constraints: one continuous 30-second take, no cuts, the same character face, hair and outfit from start to finish, natural physics and smooth motion, no on-screen text or watermark.
