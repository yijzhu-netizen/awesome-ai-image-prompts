## How to use

1. **Pick a prompt** and copy it from the code block.
2. **Fill the placeholders.** Replace anything in square brackets, like `[PRODUCT]`, `[BRAND]` or `[NAME]`.
3. **Add your reference photo** when the prompt says "uploaded photo" or "selfie". Use a sharp, well-lit, front-facing photo: the better the reference, the more the result looks like you.
4. **Paste it into an image or video tool.** Image prompts work in tools such as [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/), [Google Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/), [Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com/); video prompts work in image-to-video tools such as [Kling](https://klingai.com/), [Runway](https://runwayml.com/), [Hailuo](https://hailuoai.video/), [Pika](https://pika.art/).
5. **Use the negative prompt** (click "Negative prompt" under a prompt) in tools that have a negative-prompt field, or add "Avoid: …" to the end of the prompt in tools that don't.
6. **Iterate.** Change one thing at a time (light, outfit, background) and regenerate. For a consistent set, reuse the same wording for the subject and style in every prompt.

### Face swap (consent first)

**Read before you start:**

- Only use **your own face**, or the face of someone who has clearly agreed to this specific use.
- No celebrities or other real people without consent, and no impersonation: never make it look like someone said, did or endorsed something.
- Never use face swaps for fraud, scams, fake IDs, harassment, revenge or political deception.
- No sexual or nude content of anyone, and **never** any image involving minors.
- Label AI-edited images when you share them. Many platforms and jurisdictions require it, and most AI tools forbid non-consensual face swaps.

## Responsible use

- **Real people need consent.** Only use your own photos, or photos of people who have clearly agreed. No celebrities, no impersonation, no making anyone appear to say or endorse something.
- **No NSFW.** No sexual or nude content of anyone, and never any image involving minors.
- **No deception.** Don't use AI images for fake IDs, official documents, scams or misleading product listings. For passports, visas and ID cards, use a real photo that meets the official rules.
- **Label AI edits** when you share them, and follow each tool's terms of use.
- **Respect brands and artists.** Prompts here describe original styles and scenes; don't use them to copy trademarks or a living artist's work.
